honestly which is funnier — Arrested Development or 30 Rock?

Hard one. Arrested Development rewards rewatches more — the running gags pay off across seasons. 30 Rock is denser per minute, more jokes-per-square-inch.

If you want something you can quote forever, AD. If you want something you can dip into for 22 minutes, 30 Rock.

ok adding both to the list. is abbott elementary in the same league?

Different lineage — workplace mockumentary, more in the Office / Parks & Rec tradition. Quinta Brunson keeps it warm where 30 Rock goes cold and absurdist. Critically it's been one of the most-praised network comedies of the last decade.

switching topics — flying into JFK June 12, leaving June 19. first real NYC trip. where should we stay?

Depends what you want the week to feel like. A few solid bases:

West Village / Greenwich Village — quiet streets, walkable to almost everything, classic NYC.
Lower East Side — younger energy, food and bars at every step.
Williamsburg — across the river but easy on the L, a different kind of trip.

Want me to look at hotels in one of those?

west village. and what about food — i want at least one really memorable meal

Three tiers depending on how much of a thing you want it to be:

Splurge: Via Carota (no res, get there early) or Don Angie. Both West Village.
Hard reservation but worth it: Carbone, Lilia.
Walk-in spectacular: Raoul's, Joseph Leonard.

one more dumb question — am i underdressing if i wear sneakers in the village in june?

No. The village basically runs on sneakers. The only places you'd feel underdressed are a handful of old-school Italian spots and the fancier hotel bars — and even there you'd be fine in clean white sneakers.
